# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).

We are committed to a harassment-free, welcoming, and respectful community for
everyone. Be kind, assume good faith, critique ideas not people, and help newcomers.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, personal
attacks, and sustained disruption. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject
contributions and comments that violate this code, and may ban repeat offenders.

Report concerns privately to the maintainer
[Yash Sharma](https://www.linkedin.com/in/yashsharmaa/). All reports are handled
discreetly.
